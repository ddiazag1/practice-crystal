For grown-ups

Each practice makes the crystal a little bigger than the time before: 1% better, then 1% better than that. That's compounding: growth builds on growth.

1% a day for a year makes it about 38× bigger. Only 3 days a week: about 5×. Twice a day: about 1,400×.

The race: "Once a week" gets the same total practice as every day, crammed into one session. One big session counts like ~2.6 days (√7, diminishing returns), and a little is forgotten in between, so even the smallest daily habit beats it.

Money works the same way, only slower: about 7% a year doubles every ~10 years. Slow, slow, then BOOM.

My real crystal (📅 button) tracks actual practice: tap once a day after practicing and the crystal grows by that day's %. It's stored only in this browser, nothing is uploaded. Tap a kid's name again to rename; clear the name to delete.
