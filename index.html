The Practice Crystal 💎
Day 0 · tiny as an ant

For grown-ups

Each practice day the crystal gets a little bigger than the day before: 1% better, then 1% better than that. That's compounding: growth builds on growth.

1% a day for a year makes it about 38× bigger. Practicing only 3 days a week makes it about 5×. Fewer days doesn't just mean a little less; the gap gets huge.

Money works the same way, only slower: about 7% a year doubles every ~10 years. Slow, slow, then BOOM.
