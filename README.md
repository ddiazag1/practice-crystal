# The Practice Crystal

A tap-to-play crystal that shows kids how daily practice compounds.

Live: https://ddiazag1.github.io/practice-crystal/

- **Grow:** pick the days, % per practice and days per week, then watch it grow from an atom past a germ, the Earth, the Sun… Tap the scene to skip to the end. The results show a growth chart (slow, slow, BOOM) and the day it passed each size.
- **Race:** twice a day vs every day, every other day, or cramming once a week, side by side, with a size ladder so even a far-behind racer is readable.
- **My real crystal:** tap "I practiced today" once a day per kid; see the streak, a 5-week calendar and the next doubling. Saved only in this browser.

- **💰 Coins** (switch at the top of the settings, or `coins.html`): the same idea with money. A treasure grows from coins to chests to a dragon's hoard, with an exact live coin count split into "you put in" vs "treasure grew", a weekly allowance, and races (start early vs late, save vs spend half, piggy bank vs growing).

Two self-contained pages (`index.html`, `coins.html`), no build step.
